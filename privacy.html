At UNS Creations, your privacy is not an afterthought — it is a commitment. This Privacy Policy explains in clear, honest language how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you shop with us or interact with our website and services. Please read it carefully. By using our services, you agree to the practices described here.

Section 01

Introduction

UNS Creations ("we", "us", "our") is a women's fashion brand based in India. We operate an online store and related services. This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors, customers, and users of our website and services.

This policy is compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and other applicable data protection laws. If you are accessing our services from the European Union or other jurisdictions, additional protections may apply.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide you with an excellent shopping experience. This includes:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address when you place an order or create an account.
  • Payment Data: We do not store your payment card details. Payments are processed securely through certified third-party payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay, PhonePe).
  • Order & Purchase History: Details of products you have ordered, returned, or enquired about.
  • Communication Records: Messages you send us via our contact form, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Device & Technical Data: Your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited — collected automatically when you use our website.
  • Marketing Preferences: Your opt-in or opt-out preferences for newsletters and promotional communications.
  • User-Generated Content: Reviews, ratings, or photos you voluntarily submit.
Section 03

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information only for legitimate purposes that are necessary to operate our business and serve you well:

  • To process and fulfil your orders and manage returns or exchanges.
  • To communicate order confirmations, shipping updates, and delivery notifications.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To send you marketing and promotional emails — only if you have opted in to receive them.
  • To personalise your shopping experience and show you relevant products.
  • To improve our website, detect fraud, and ensure security.
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations under Indian law.
  • To analyse aggregate, anonymised data to understand purchasing trends and improve our services.

We will never sell your personal data to any third party for their own commercial use. Your information is yours, and we treat it accordingly.

Section 04

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your data with trusted partners only where necessary to deliver our services. All third parties we work with are required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

  • Delivery & Logistics Partners: We share your name, address, and phone number with our shipping partners (e.g., Delhivery, Blue Dart) solely to fulfil your delivery.
  • Payment Processors: Your payment details are handled by secure, PCI-DSS compliant gateways. We do not retain full card details.
  • Technology Providers: Service providers who operate our website, email platform, and analytics tools, operating under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Authorities: We may disclose your information where required by law, court order, or to protect the safety and rights of our customers and the public.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Section 05

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to enhance your experience. We use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function (e.g., your shopping cart and login session). These cannot be disabled without affecting functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. Data is anonymised and aggregated (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings such as currency, language, or region.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect your shopping experience.

Section 06

Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. These include SSL encryption, secure hosting environments, and access control policies.

We retain your personal data for as long as is necessary to:

  • Maintain your account and service history.
  • Comply with applicable legal and accounting obligations (typically 7 years for financial records under Indian law).
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

When data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it.

Section 07

Your Rights Under the DPDP Act

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights as a Data Principal:

  • Right of Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is being processed.
  • Right to Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data where we no longer have a legitimate basis to retain it.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to raise a complaint with us if you believe your data has been mishandled. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. This will not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.
  • Right to Nominate: You may nominate another individual to exercise your data rights in the event of your incapacity or death.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@unscreations.com. We will respond within 30 calendar days.

Section 08

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@unscreations.com and we will take prompt action to delete such data.

Section 09

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as our social media profiles, payment gateways, or partner brands). Once you click on an external link, you leave our website and this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party websites and encourage you to review their own policies before sharing any personal information.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal obligations. When we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page, and — where appropriate — by sending you an email notification.

Your continued use of our services after the effective date of any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
